Café Fujisan no Megumi, Fujikawa Rakuza

Indoor observation-lounge café with views of Mount Fuji and the Fuji River.

Route: T. Tomei Fujikawa SA eastbound; walk into adjoining Fujikawa Rakuza, fourth floor

Extra driving: On the expressway: 0 min / 0 km exit/re-entry diversion. On the expressway only when using Tomei eastbound. From Shin-Tomei, switching corridors is additional travel, not a zero-detour stop.

Time to allow: 30–60 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Warm scenic break suited to grandparents; keep twins with adults at windows and during floor changes. Indoor lounge; views depend on cloud. SA access enters Rakuza at third-floor level; café is on the fourth. Lift route should be confirmed on arrival.

Toilets / baby facilities: Shared Rakuza/SA toilets; nearest accessible toilet not independently checked. SA/Rakuza nursing-room location and availability unverified.

Hours (Japan time): Café 10:00–17:00. Closed: Rakuza may close for facility inspections; exact 20 December operation unverified. Exact travel-date operation unverified. Use this current café rather than the old Fujikawa eastbound Starbucks, which is listed as closed.

Food budget: Planning budget: coffee/cake JPY 500–1,200 (≈ SGD 4.05–9.72 · ≈ MYR 12.85–30.84); exact prices unverified. Admission / parking: Café purchases extra; observation lounge access/any associated attraction fees should be checked.
