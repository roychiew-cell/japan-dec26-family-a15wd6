Ise Grand Shrine, Inner Shrine / Okage Yokocho

Major shrine visit with the neighbouring historic food-and-shopping streets.

Route: A/B/C-A/C-B. Southward backtrack from VISON; Ise-Nishi IC for Inner Shrine

Extra driving: A/B/C-A/C-B: +40 min / +44 km Large sightseeing diversion. Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
A/B/C-A/C-B: Seiwa-Taki IC southbound → Ise-Nishi IC → Inner Shrine → Ise-Nishi IC northbound.

Time to allow: 120–180 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: A substantial outing in the opposite direction; stroller/carrier helpful, but gravel and crowds are tiring. Mostly outdoors; indoor restaurants and shops offer intermittent shelter. Shrine operator says about 20 min each way from Uji Bridge to the main sanctuary; gravel and steps.

Toilets / baby facilities: Public toilets in the shrine/visitor precinct; exact closest accessible route unverified. Visitor support facilities in Ise exist; exact nursing room for this walking circuit unverified.

Hours (Japan time): Inner Shrine December 05:00–17:00. Okage Yokocho shops have seasonal hours; exact December 2026 shopping hours unverified. Closed: Shrine open daily; individual shops have separate schedules. Exact travel-date operation unverified. Maps targets the Inner Shrine only. Okage Yokocho is an adjacent extension, not a second distant stop.

Food budget: Planning budget: snacks JPY 300–800 (≈ SGD 2.43–6.48 · ≈ MYR 7.71–20.56); casual meal JPY 1,000–2,500 (≈ SGD 8.10–20.25 · ≈ MYR 25.70–64.25); unverified. Admission / parking: Shrine admission free; parking and purchases extra.
