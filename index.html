Luggage forwarding

Japan's door-to-door luggage delivery. Hand a suitcase to a hotel front desk or convenience store and it is waiting at the next hotel, usually next day.

How to sendEasiest: the hotel front desk fills in the slip and hands the bag to Yamato. Or any 7-Eleven or FamilyMart counter. No app or reservation needed.
What to writeNext hotel's name, address and phone, the name on the hotel booking (Roy's) and the check-in date.
TimingUsually next day between these cities. Late December is peak season, so allow 2 days.
PayPer bag, at the counter, cash or card. Size is length + width + height.
