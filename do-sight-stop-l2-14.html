Miho no Matsubara / Miho Shirube

Pine grove and Fuji-view shoreline with the small indoor Miho Shirube visitor centre.

Route: T/S. Tomei Shimizu IC → Miho peninsula; Shin-Tomei requires a connector/local approach

Extra driving: S: +65 min / +34 km
T: +50 min / +15 km Large sightseeing diversion. Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
S: Shimizu JCT connector → Shimizu IC → Miho → Shimizu-Ihara IC eastbound.
T: Shimizu IC → Miho → Shimizu IC eastbound.

Time to allow: 45–90 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Short grove visit possible; exposed beach and uneven ground are less comfortable for grandparents in December. Predominantly outdoor grove/beach; small indoor visitor centre. Pine paths and loose sand/pebbles make a stroller difficult beyond the paved approach.

Toilets / baby facilities: Visitor-centre/public toilet details not independently checked. Dedicated nursing-room availability unverified.

Hours (Japan time): Grove/shoreline: no gate hours; daylight visit advised. Miho Shirube 09:00–16:30. Closed: Miho Shirube published as open every day; natural area has no routine closure. Exact travel-date operation unverified.

Food budget: Planning budget: snacks/drinks JPY 300–800 (≈ SGD 2.43–6.48 · ≈ MYR 7.71–20.56); local shops and prices unverified. Admission / parking: Visitor centre free; shop purchases extra; parking terms not independently checked.
