Planning notes and evidence
  • Takashimaya Osaka, 6th floor (baby and kids): a small Sanrio corner, open 10:00 to 20:00 daily (read 15 Nov), about 7 minutes on foot from the hotel (10 to 12 with strollers), baby room on the same floor. Size and Hello Kitty range unknown. Map ↗
  • Cinnamoroll Cafe, opposite the Gift Gate: it is Cinnamoroll, not Hello Kitty. Weekdays 11:00 to 21:00 (read 15 Nov); takeaway and the goods corner need no booking; no kids' chairs. Map ↗
  • Sanrio House cafe booth, Nagoya PARCO (Sakae): Hello Kitty crepes, JPY 1,680 (≈ SGD 13.61 · ≈ MYR 43.18) each. Online booking only: book by 15:00 the day before, 60-minute seats, eat-in, strollers folded. 4 people at most per booking, so 7 need two bookings. Seats for 19 Dec not checked yet. Only Sat 19 Dec from about 17:00, by two taxis (15 to 20 minutes). Sun 20 Dec is not advised: we would reach the lake house around dark. Map ↗ Booking page (TableCheck, Japanese) ↗
  • Sanrio Tokyo Solamachi (Skytree), 4th floor: 10:00 to 21:00 daily (read 15 Nov), baby rooms on the same floor; about 30 to 35 minutes each way (estimate). Map ↗
  • Sanrio Asakusa: 10:30 to 18:00 daily (read 15 Nov), two floors and no lift known; the giant Hello Kitty front is not confirmed for December; about 30 to 40 minutes each way (estimate). Map ↗
  • Sanrio Characters Garden Cafe, Ueno: Hello Kitty sofa seat. Mon to Thu and Sun 10:00 to 19:00, Fri and Sat 10:00 to 21:00 (operator page, read 15 Nov). The cafe recommends booking but does not require it. Stroller entry not stated. About 20 to 25 minutes each way (estimate). Map ↗

    • Some Hello Kitty items are sold only in one region (Nagoya, Hamamatsu, Mie and Mt Fuji designs exist). No source confirms stock now: ask in the shop.

    More Sanrio shops exist and were left out to keep this short: Sanrio NISHIGINZA (Ginza flagship, no lift listed), the Sanrio corner at Takashimaya Nihombashi (5th floor), Sanrio Ueno and Sanrio Shinsaibashi (Osaka), and the districts of Umeda, Harajuku, Ikebukuro, Odaiba and Shibuya.

    Not on this route: Sanrio Harmonyland is in Oita, Kyushu, far from this route. Universal Studios Japan lists Hello Kitty areas but is not on our plan.

  • Ise-Shima: no Sanrio shop in Mie, and nothing at Okage Yokocho, VISON, Toba Aquarium or Shima Spain Village.
  • Lake Yamanaka and Mt Fuji: none; the nearest is the Gotemba outlet above.
  • Kansai Airport: nothing before security, and no Hello Kitty train to plan on (the Haruka wrap train cannot be chosen and does not run to Namba).
  • Narita Terminal 1: no Sanrio shop, so buy before the last day.
  • Singapore: only the Jewel store is confirmed. A Sanrio corner at Takashimaya (Orchard Road) and a Chinatown store appear only in third-party listings, and no Sanrio pop-up was found for December.

    • One park and the Sanrio shops on our way, in trip order.

  • Puroland: yes or no, and Fri 25 or Sat 26 Dec. Decide before the Christmas meals are booked (about Mon 30 Nov). Booking for both days is already open on the official site, and Roy makes the reservation.
  • Nagoya PARCO cafe on Sat 19 Dec (optional): two online bookings of 4 and 3 at the same time. December slots opened on 10 Nov and fill fast.
  • Jewel on Tue 15 Dec (optional): if wanted, book the Changi van for about 17:15 instead of straight after landing (the van is booked by about Wed 9 Dec).
  • Look at the official hours of every shop again about 1 Dec and 15 Dec.

    • One Hello Kitty park is on our route: Sanrio Puroland, in Tama, west of Tokyo. Along the way there are Sanrio shops in Singapore, Osaka, Nagoya, the Mt Fuji area and Tokyo. Opening hours were read on the official pages on 15 and 16 Nov 2026; anything not confirmed says so. Nothing is booked, and no Sanrio shop has posted year-end hours yet.

    Jewel is joined to Terminal 1, where the Scoot flight lands at 14:35 on Tue 15 Dec. The flights on Wed 16 and Tue 29 Dec leave before it opens.

    On Thu 17 Dec the car is collected at 12:00 from the Toyota Namba Terminal Shop, about 14 minutes on foot from the hotel (estimate). Check out first and leave the bags at the front desk: the driver collects the car while the others visit a shop, then everyone meets at the hotel to load.

    Also here

    Thu 24 Dec is the DisneySea day, Fri 25 Dec is built around the Christmas meals, and Sun 27 Dec is left empty (Disneyland dropped). That leaves Wed 23 Dec evening, Sat 26 Dec and Sun 27 Dec. If Puroland takes Sat 26, the only easy stop left is the Tokyo Station shop (any evening until 20:30); the other Tokyo shops fit on Sat 26 Dec or, if they are open that day (not checked), Sun 27 Dec.

    Hours can change, especially around New Year. Travel times are estimates: allow longer with two strollers and the grandparents.
