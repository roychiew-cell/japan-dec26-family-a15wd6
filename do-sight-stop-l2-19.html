Oshino Hakkai spring ponds

Traditional village scenery and clear Fuji-fed spring ponds.

Route: S/T/C. Oshino village, off Route 138 near the Yamanakako / Oshino approaches

Time to allow: 40–75 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Keep twins close beside water; short visit only if daylight, dry footing and grandparents are comfortable. Mostly outdoor lanes and pond edges; small shops offer limited shelter. Uneven/narrow paths and crowds. Tourism association says not all ponds can be circled by wheelchair.

Toilets / baby facilities: Prefectural tourism information lists accessible toilets. Dedicated nursing-room availability unverified.

Hours (Japan time): Public ponds have no gate/opening hours; visit in daylight. Sokonuke Pond is inside a museum with separate hours and closures. Closed: Public ponds open year-round; private shops/museum have their own schedules. Exact travel-date operation unverified. Not all eight ponds are freely accessible at all hours; the paid museum pond is a specific exception.

Food budget: Planning budget: snacks JPY 300–800 (≈ SGD 2.43–6.48 · ≈ MYR 7.71–20.56); meal JPY 1,000–2,000 (≈ SGD 8.10–16.20 · ≈ MYR 25.70–51.40); shop hours/prices unverified. Admission / parking: Public pond viewing free; private parking, museum and purchases extra.
