Sanrio Gift Gate Namba Ebisubashi

Sanrio Gift Gate なんば戎橋店

Sanrio's own two-floor gift shop on the Ebisubashi-suji shopping street, with an Ebisu-style Hello Kitty and a photo spot on the second floor.

From the hotelAbout 13 minutes on foot; 18 to 20 with strollers (estimate).

Hours11:30 to 20:00 daily (official Sanrio page, read 15 Nov).

Best slotWed 16 Dec evening, only if the twins are still going after the 04:45 start: about 18:45 to 19:45 (closes 20:00), easy to pair with dinner in Namba. Or Thu 17 Dec: the others visit from 11:30 while the driver collects the car.
