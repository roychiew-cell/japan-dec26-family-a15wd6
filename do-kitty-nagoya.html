Sanrio Takashimaya Gate Tower Mall (formerly Sanrio Gift Gate)

Sanrio タカシマヤ ゲートタワーモール店

Sanrio's own gift shop with capsule toys on level 7 of the mall at Nagoya Station, a short walk from the hotel. No booking needed.

From the hotelAbout 3 minutes on foot from the Marriott at street level; 8 to 12 minutes door to door with lifts (estimate).

Hours10:00 to 20:00 daily (official Sanrio and mall pages, read 15 Nov). No closed day or year-end notice posted for December.

Best slotSat 19 Dec after the 15:00 check-in (open to 20:00), or Sun 20 Dec from 10:00 before the drive. Expect crowds on the last Saturday before Christmas.
