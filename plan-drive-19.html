Route notes and timing estimates

Coffee, food and sightseeing · researched 17 October 2026

Timing: about 1½–2 hours driving before stops and traffic. Check out of Hatago VISON by 11:00; Nagoya hotel check-in starts at 15:00. Leaving around 11:00, one 45–60-minute meal and a 20–30-minute break may fit before 15:00 if traffic and access are kind. Coffee/bakery at VISON before checkout saves road time. Choose one modest outing, or leave earlier for LEGOLAND, Ghibli Park or Suzuka and expect a later hotel arrival. Nabana’s lights are an evening choice. Ise shrine is a backtrack south. Taki VISON Smart IC is exit-only: use an entrance such as Seiwa-Taki.

Route key: A = inland Higashi-Meihan to Nagoya; B = Isewangan bay side. C-A / C-B = Shin-Meishin bypass via Suzuka, then the corresponding inland / bay-side continuation; this bypass skips Gozaisho.
