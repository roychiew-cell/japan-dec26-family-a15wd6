Mitsui Outlet Park Jazz Dream Nagashima

Large outlet complex with food court and cafés for a flexible shopping/meal break.

Route: B/C-B. Isewangan Expressway, immediately by Wangan-Nagashima IC

Extra driving: B/C-B: +10 min / +5 km Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
B/C-B: Wangan-Nagashima IC → outlet visitor parking → Wangan-Nagashima IC eastbound. Wangan-Nagashima PA is a corridor-forcing routing anchor, with no on-site stop. Allowance is approximate because ramp/parking access can differ from staying on the mainline. C-A/C-B share the corresponding A/B roads after Yokkaichi JCT; the unchanged prefix cancels in this comparison. Select the exact continuation before using its number.

Time to allow: 60–120 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Convenient toilets and lunch; Saturday shopping/parking can add delay. Outdoor shopping lanes; indoor stores, cafés and food court. Large site; choose one zone to limit walking and keep grandparents warm.

Toilets / baby facilities: Toilets and accessible toilets shown on the operator’s floor map. Baby rest rooms and changing rooms shown on the floor map.

Hours (Japan time): Shops 10:00–20:00; Saturday food court 10:30–21:00 (L.O.20:30); restaurant hours vary. Closed: No fixed closing days; temporary closures still possible. Exact travel-date operation unverified.

Food budget: Planning budget: food court JPY 1,000–2,000 (≈ SGD 8.10–16.20 · ≈ MYR 25.70–51.40); café JPY 500–1,000 (≈ SGD 4.05–8.10 · ≈ MYR 12.85–25.70); unverified. Admission / parking: Shopping admission free; current parking fees/validation not verified.
