Rokkaen historic house and garden

Western/Japanese historic residence and garden beside Kuwana’s riverfront.

Route: A/B/C-A/C-B. Kuwana IC on Higashi-Meihan, or Wangan-Kuwana IC on Isewangan

Extra driving: A/C-A: +20 min / +6 km
B/C-B: +20 min / +8 km Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
A/C-A: Kuwana IC → Rokkaen → Kuwana-Higashi IC northbound rejoin. C-A/C-B share the corresponding A/B roads after Yokkaichi JCT; the unchanged prefix cancels in this comparison. Select the exact continuation before using its number.
B/C-B: Kuwana IC → Rokkaen → Wangan-Kuwana IC eastbound (corridor change included). Wangan-Nagashima PA is a corridor-forcing routing anchor, with no on-site stop. C-A/C-B share the corresponding A/B roads after Yokkaichi JCT; the unchanged prefix cancels in this comparison. Select the exact continuation before using its number.

Time to allow: 45–75 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: A quieter cultural stop, but check mobility access if grandparents avoid steps. Historic indoor rooms plus outdoor garden. Expect shoe removal, thresholds and historic-house constraints; shorten garden walk if cold.

Toilets / baby facilities: Toilet accessibility and location not verified. Dedicated nursing room unverified.

Hours (Japan time): 09:00–17:00; last admission 16:00. Closed: Mondays (next weekday if Monday is a holiday); 29 December–3 January. Exact travel-date operation unverified.

Food budget: Food is optional nearby; current café availability/prices unverified. Admission / parking: Published adult admission JPY 460 (≈ SGD 3.73 · ≈ MYR 11.82); primary-school age and younger free with an accompanying adult.
