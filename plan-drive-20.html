Nagoya
Expressway past Mt Fuji
Lake Yamanaka house

About 3½–4 hours' drive. The house opens at 15:00, so we leave Nagoya mid-morning. It is about 1,000 m up, so there may be snow or ice on the last stretch.

Route notes and timing estimates

Timing: about 3½–4 hours driving before stops and traffic. 15:00 is when the house opens, not an arrival deadline. For an arrival around 15:00, finish checkout and loading around 08:35–09:00 and depart roughly 09:00. Allow 4 hours driving + 45 minutes for a motorway meal + 15 minutes for a toilet/coffee break + 20 minutes child-seat handling + 40 minutes traffic buffer = 6 hours. Local detour driving is extra; serious congestion, snow or ice can exceed that buffer. Leaving at the 12:00 checkout limit puts even the unstopped drive around 15:30–16:00.

Sightseeing pushes arrival later: replacing the 45-minute meal with a 90-minute Kakegawa Kachoen visit/lunch, its 45-minute Shin-Tomei detour and 10 extra minutes handling adds about 100 minutes: the example arrival becomes 16:40. Its Tomei detour is smaller. Avoid stacking sightseeing, Numazu Port, shopping and west Fuji. The house is about 1,000 m high: confirm winter tyres, road conditions and the final house access, and keep the ascent in daylight where practical. Chuo is a longer, higher winter alternative; its baseline is not the 3½–4-hour estimate.

Route key: S = default Shin-Tomei via Shin-Gotemba; T = Tomei alternative via Gotemba; C = northern Chuo via Okaya, Kofu and Otsuki; F = large west-Fuji sightseeing diversion. Switching corridors adds travel; use the number for the approach you choose.
