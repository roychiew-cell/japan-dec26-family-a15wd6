Nihondaira Yume Terrace

Hilltop Fuji/Suruga Bay observation building, deck and tea lounge.

Route: T/S. Tomei Shimizu IC or Shizuoka IC; Shin-Tomei Shin-Shizuoka IC is farther

Extra driving: S: +65 min / +27 km
T: +45 min / +15 km Large sightseeing diversion. Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
S: Shin-Shizuoka IC → Nihondaira → Shimizu-Ihara IC eastbound rejoin.
T: Nihondaira-Kunozan Smart IC → Nihondaira → Shimizu IC eastbound rejoin.

Time to allow: 45–75 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Good when Fuji is clear; indoor lounge helps grandparents, but wind/cloud may reduce the value of the diversion. Indoor exhibition/lounge and exposed outdoor viewing corridor. Parking-to-building walk; hillside access road and exact lift route need checking.

Toilets / baby facilities: Toilets available; accessible location details not independently checked. Dedicated nursing-room availability unverified.

Hours (Japan time): Sunday indoor facility 09:00–17:00; outdoor observation corridor open all day under published rules. Closed: Second Tuesday (next weekday if holiday); 26–31 December. Exact travel-date operation unverified. Older guide pages still call the building free; the operator announced paid admission from 1 October 2026.

Food budget: Planning budget: tea/coffee and cake JPY 600–1,200 (≈ SGD 4.86–9.72 · ≈ MYR 15.42–30.84); unverified. Admission / parking: From October 2026: adult/high-school observation-building entry JPY 100 (≈ SGD 0.81 · ≈ MYR 2.57); preschool free; outdoor corridor free (prefectural tourism listing).
