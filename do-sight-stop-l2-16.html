Mt. Fuji World Heritage Centre, Shizuoka

Indoor Fuji exhibition with a spiralling simulated mountain ascent and viewing floor.

Route: S/T. Fujinomiya beside the Sengen Taisha district; access from Shin-Fuji / Fuji IC

Extra driving: S: +35 min / +16 km
T: +40 min / +21 km Large sightseeing diversion. Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
S: Shin-Fuji IC → heritage centre → Shin-Fuji IC eastbound.
T: Fuji IC → heritage centre → Fuji IC eastbound.

Time to allow: 60–90 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Good winter indoor alternative near the shrine; combine parking/walking rather than counting both as separate long outings. Indoor museum with an optional outdoor reflecting-pool view. The main exhibition is a long sloping route; confirm lift access for grandparents who prefer to avoid it.

Toilets / baby facilities: Toilet/accessibility details not independently checked in this pass. Dedicated nursing-room availability unverified.

Hours (Japan time): 09:00–17:00; last admission 16:30. Closed: 2026 list: December 1–4, 15 and 27–31 closed; Sunday 20 December is outside those closures. 2026 closure calendar checked; regular hours remain provisional.

Food budget: On-site café menu and availability unverified; planning budget JPY 600–1,200 (≈ SGD 4.86–9.72 · ≈ MYR 15.42–30.84) if operating. Admission / parking: Published permanent exhibit: adult JPY 500 (≈ SGD 4.05 · ≈ MYR 12.85); under 15 and age 70+ free under Japanese rules, with proof where required; special exhibitions may differ.
