Shiraito Falls, Fujinomiya

Wide spring-fed waterfall curtain and the neighbouring Otodome Falls.

Route: S/T. Leave at Shin-Fuji / Fuji IC, then Route 139 to Shiraito; optional west-Fuji corridor

Time to allow: 45–75 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Grandparents can stay at upper viewpoint; wet steps and December ice make the lower path a poor default for this family. Outdoors; shops provide limited shelter. About 5 min from parking to Otodome, another 5 min to Shiraito. Step-free route reaches the upper viewpoint; around 100 steps down to the falls basin.

Toilets / baby facilities: Public toilets by visitor parking; accessible toilets listed in prefectural tourism information. Infant facilities listed, but dedicated nursing room not confirmed.

Hours (Japan time): Natural falls: daylight visit. Official winter car park 09:00–16:30, entry up to 30 min before closing. Closed: Tourism association lists car park as open year-round. Exact travel-date operation unverified. The Fujinomiya town qualifier is essential: there are other Shiraito waterfalls in Japan.

Food budget: Planning budget: snacks/light food JPY 500–1,500 (≈ SGD 4.05–12.15 · ≈ MYR 12.85–38.55); winter tenant hours and prices unverified. Admission / parking: Falls free; official standard-car parking JPY 500 (≈ SGD 4.05 · ≈ MYR 12.85).
