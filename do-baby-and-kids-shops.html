    Filled dot: local baby chain, drugstore, department store or supermarket. Ring: international name or outlet mall. Dark square: our hotel. Tap a pin for the hours and its exact Google Maps link.

    Baby Kingdom showroom (83 Kaki Bukit Ave 1) and Baby's Hyperstore (69 Kaki Bukit Ave 1) are big but in the east, and close at 19:00. Probably not worth the trip for a one-night stay.

    The same stores serve both hotels (Citadines Connect City Centre and Holiday Inn Express Clarke Quay): Google Maps lists Marina Square and Plaza Singapura among the results for both hotels. Distance from each hotel was not measured.

    Buy the bulk here: it is the strongest choice found on the whole route. Buy enough for about 5 to 6 days here and top up on Sun 20 Dec.

    There is no Akachan Honpo in Mie, so the choice is thinner. Check what is left before leaving Osaka. None of these distances from VISON was measured.

    No large baby store was found at the station. For top-ups, the drugstores around the station are open late; the full store is across town.

    For the three-night lake stay few shops were found, so stock up on Sun 20 Dec on the way, before reaching the lake. Numazu and Nagaizumi may be on or near the way to Gotemba; the route and detour were not measured.

    Google Maps shows only boutiques right around the hotel. Make one trip to Ikebukuro for the big store and use the drugstores around Tokyo Station for top-ups.

    Baby shops at every stop, a mix of local chains and international names. Sources checked 22 Nov 2026; stock was not checked.

    Opening hours are from Google Maps on 22 Nov 2026 or, where it says official, from the chain's own store page. Stock and prices were not checked, and December hours may change. Akachan Honpo's English tax-free page says the buyer must do the paperwork in person and that a 1.1% fee applies.

  • Japanese baby chains (Akachan Honpo, Nishimatsuya, Birthday, Mizutani): the shops to try first for formula, diapers, bottles, baby food and gear. Each store's range was not checked.
  • Drugstores (Sugi, Daikoku, Matsumoto Kiyoshi, Welcia): usually carry diapers and milk powder (not checked store by store) and stay open late. Handy for quick top-ups.
  • Toys R Us / Babies R Us (international; reports of 30 Sep 2026 say the business is being taken over by Don Quijote's parent company and its stores are expected to stay open): a toy shop with a baby section for gear, clothes and care items. One guide says the baby side also sells baby food and formula; that was not confirmed for any of these stores, so use the Japanese chains or drugstores for formula and diapers.
  • Mothercare (UK) and the Singapore chains Kiddy Palace and Motherswork: gear, bottles, clothes. Formula stock was not checked.
