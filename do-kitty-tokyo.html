Sanrio Tokyo Character Street Store (Tokyo Station First Avenue)

Sanrio 東京キャラクターストリート店

Sanrio's own Hello Kitty and character gift shop inside Tokyo Station, on the street-level floor of First Avenue. The closest stop to the hotel.

From the hotelAbout 4 to 8 minutes on foot at street level, without crossing the station (estimate).

Hours10:00 to 20:30 daily (official Sanrio and First Avenue pages, read 15 Nov). No year-end hours posted yet.

Best slotWed 23 Dec after the 15:00 car return and check-in (open to 20:30), or any evening until 20:30. A 20 to 30 minute stop. Not Mon 28 Dec: flight day.
